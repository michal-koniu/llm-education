## Weakest points — check these first

1. **Lawyers' hourly rates** come only from marketing pages. They drive the value ceiling (part 03). Replace
   them with what your interviewees tell you.
2. **Support-hour cost, assistant cost, partner margins** are assumptions. Get one real quote for each.
3. **The art. 113(13) VAT point** in part 05 is from memory, not from this research.
4. **Hardware prices** are a one-day Ceneo snapshot; NVIDIA changed its reference price once in 2026 already.
5. **Van Westendorp conventions** differ between tools; this course's tool uses the "not cheap / not expensive"
   crossings for PMC and PME.
6. Several vendor prices (Legalis base, Noa Notes, LEX Expert AI, Gaius-Lex) are **not published**.
