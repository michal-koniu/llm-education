## Phase 0: before launch (30 Oct to 18 Nov)

**Goal:** everything ready except the content. Nothing published that comes from leaks or review copies.

| Days | Human | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Oct to 4 Nov | Pick the vertical (for example: missables plus one collectible family plus vehicles, or settings and accessibility). Read the live Rockstar video policy and mod guidelines, YouTube monetisation policy, Google spam policy. Decide on own voice or disclosed synthetic voice (never a clone of a character or actor). Register channel, domain and Discord under a brand without "GTA" or "Grand Theft Auto" in it; descriptive use in titles and text is normal | A0 orchestrator, job queue and approval tokens. A10 starts snapshotting the policy pages. Glossary skeleton for A9 |
| 5 to 11 Nov | Design the data schema with the agents: record types, fields, status, source. Draw (or commission) an original vector base map style; the real map comes after launch. Write the site's about page, not-affiliated line and spoiler policy | A5 builds the tracker UI and page templates against dummy data. A4 submission bot on a test server. A1 tested on old GTA V or other footage |
| 9 Nov | Watch the earnings call | A10 summarises |
| 12 to 18 Nov | Full dry run: capture one hour of any game, run it through A1 to A8 end to end, publish nothing. Fix the bottlenecks. Optional: one or two commentary videos on official material only (react, break down; no raw reposting [15]). This is saturated, so treat it as a test of the pipeline, not a growth lever | All agents in rehearsal. A7 thumbnail and title templates |
| 18 Nov | Embargo lifts. Read reviews for hints about structure (missables, chapters), not for content to copy | A10 tracks which head terms the big publishers take |

**Do not:** use leaked material, post review-copy footage, promise "GTA 6 money" content, run "free code" giveaways, or buy followers to look established.

## Phase 1: launch fortnight (19 Nov to 2 Dec, days 1 to 14)

**Goal:** be first and right on a narrow vertical. Get the tracker live with real data within 72 hours.

| Days | Human (about 3 to 4.5 h/day, more on launch weekend) | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Play with capture running, voice notes on, pins placed. Go for breadth in your vertical, avoid story spoilers in public outputs | A1 ingests overnight; A3 drafts records; A2 flags spoilers |
| By day 3 | Verify the first 30 to 100 records (H3). Publish the tracker with a visible "verified / unverified" status | A5 deploys; A10 starts traffic and rank tracking |
| 3 to 7 | First 3 to 5 long videos in your vertical. Record narration from A6 scripts you edited | A6 drafts, A7 rough cuts and Shorts candidates, A8 schedules after H4 |
| 5 to 14 | Open the Discord for submissions. Answer people personally in the first two weeks: early trust is the asset | A4 runs intake and dedup; A11 routes corrections to A3 |
| 8 to 14 | Add English long-tail guides with first-hand detail. Add affiliate links, labelled, for Black Friday | A5 renders guide pages from records; A10 reports which pages get impressions |

**Targets by day 14 (estimate, for a base-case path):** 150 to 500 verified records; 6 to 10 long videos; 20 to 40 Shorts; 200 to 1,000 Discord members; first search impressions on long-tail pages. If you have none of these, the plan has a problem, not just the market.

**Expect:** you will not rank for head terms. Large guide sites, wikis and map businesses will own them from day 1. That is normal.

## Phase 3: weeks 7 to 10 (31 Dec to 27 Jan, days 43 to 70)

**Goal:** get into the Partner Program before the bar rises, and get the site into an ad network.

- **Partner Program push.** If the projection shows you will cross 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours only after 31 Jan, shift effort to the long videos with the highest average view duration. Do not buy views or subscribers; that risks the channel. If you miss the date, the 8,000-hour bar applies (reported) [30, 31].
- **Ads.** Apply to an ad network once traffic meets its threshold. Start with a light ad layout so the tracker remains usable.
- **Languages.** Grow to 6 to 8 languages only where the first three showed traffic. Keep native sample review.
- **Paid tier.** If the Discord and tracker have regular users, test an ad-free synced tier at a small price. It sells features only: it never gates GTA footage or screenshots, and nothing is tied to Rockstar currency or items [14, 16]. Disclose any sponsor.
- **Cash engine.** S1: keep clients who pay on time; drop the rest. S3: second or third job; P3: ship the first script on Tebex. S2: keep or drop based on time.

## Phase 4: weeks 11 to 13 (28 Jan to 16 Feb, days 71 to 90)

**Goal:** decide what to keep, and position for the second wave.

- **By 31 Jan:** apply to the Partner Program if eligible; accept any new terms.
- **Early Feb:** Take-Two's earnings may bring sales numbers and PC or online signals. Have the schema ready for new categories (mods, online businesses, PC settings).
- **Day 90 review:** apply the kill criteria below. Cut the weakest workstream; double the strongest.

---

## Kill and double-down criteria

All thresholds are judgement calls (estimate). They exist so you decide on data, not hope.

| Check | Day 30 (18 Dec) | Day 60 (17 Jan) | Day 90 (16 Feb) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tracker sessions / month | ≥ 10k, else cut scope to one category | ≥ 30k, else stop adding languages | ≥ 50k and growing, else switch to maintenance mode |
| Channel subscribers | ≥ 300 | ≥ 800, on course for the 31 Jan bar | In the Partner Program, or a clear path to 8,000 hours within 6 months; else reduce to 1 video a week |
| Average view duration | ≥ 35% on long videos | Stable or rising | — |
| Discord active submitters / week | ≥ 10 | ≥ 25 | ≥ 40 |
| Verified records | ≥ 300 | ≥ 800 | Coverage of your vertical ≥ 90% |
| Policy events | Any strike or claim → stop and review the workflow that caused it | Same | Same |
| Cash engine | S1: ≥ 1 paying client? S3/S2: first paid job? | S1: ≥ 3 clients; P3: first script shipped? | S1: ≥ $500/month or stop; P3: ≥ 20 sales, else stop |

**Double down** on whichever of tracker or channel clearly leads at day 60. In the most likely case, one of them works modestly and the other does not.
