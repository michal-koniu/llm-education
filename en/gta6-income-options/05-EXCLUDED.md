# 05. Excluded, and why

As of 30 October 2026. These options are not rated, because they break Rockstar or Take-Two terms, platform rules, payment-provider rules or the law, or because the product does not exist. Several of them are heavily promoted around big launches. The "Risk" column says what typically happens to people who try.

Numbers in brackets point to `SOURCES.md`.

## Breaks Rockstar / Take-Two terms

| Idea | Why excluded | Main risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheats, mod menus, trainers, "GTA 6 cheat" subscriptions | Banned by the Mod Guidelines and Terms of Service; cheat menus that touch official online services are named | Account bans for users, legal action against sellers, payment processors drop the merchant | [10, 11] |
| Boosting, "money drops", account levelling or "recovery" services | Terms of Service; Take-Two has sued an account and currency marketplace | Bans, chargebacks, lawsuits | [26] |
| Selling in-game currency, items or accounts | Virtual items have no cash value; commercial markets are banned; Creator Platform terms forbid selling virtual currency | Same as above | [16, 26] |
| Leaked or pre-release footage, review-copy footage before embargo, "early access" unboxings | Rockstar's policy bans pre-release and leaked footage; Take-Two pursues leaks; review copies carry embargo contracts | Strikes, channel termination, legal action, loss of future access | [13, 14] |
| AI-generated "GTA 6 gameplay", fake leaks, character renders presented as real | Take-Two has issued copyright strikes against channels posting AI fakes; separately, an AI "leak" on X reached 8M views before its creator deleted it after a backlash | DMCA strikes, termination, fraud complicity | [22, 23] |
| Re-uploading official trailers or raw cutscenes | Rockstar: "reposting the raw unedited video is not allowed"; isolated cutscenes and endings banned | Takedowns, strikes | [14, 15] |
| Any paid tier that gates GTA footage or screenshots (members-only GTA videos, paywalled galleries, screenshot packs); promoting your own merch with GTA footage | Rockstar's footage policy names memberships that sell access to footage and merch promotion. Paid tiers may sell features only: sync, ad-free, tools, community roles | Takedowns | [14] |
| AI voice clones of GTA characters or their voice actors; AI likeness of real cast members | Mod Guidelines ban reusing Rockstar voice performances and real people's likeness without written consent; voice and likeness rights also apply outside the game | Takedowns, claims by performers, platform removal | [10, 11] |
| "GTA", "Grand Theft Auto" or Rockstar marks in a domain, channel, app or brand name (e.g. a "GTA 6 Tracker" app) | Trademark use that implies affiliation; invites domain-dispute and trademark complaints and app-store removal. Descriptive use in titles and page text ("a map for GTA 6") is normal | Loss of the domain, app or channel name | [10, 11] |
| Tracing the official in-game map or official art for a map base layer | A close tracing is arguably a derivative work. Draw from your own observations; even then, keep a takedown plan | Takedown of the map | (estimate) |
| Unlicensed merch using "Grand Theft Auto", GTA 6 logos, characters, key art or in-game brands | Trademark and copyright; Take-Two enforces against fan projects | Marketplace account termination, legal letters | [25] |
| Crypto, NFT or memecoin tie-ins; "crypto-backed RP servers" | Banned in Mod Guidelines and Creator Platform terms; RP-server NFT ban since 2022 | Store closure, funds held, platform bans | [10, 16, 27, 78] |
| Loot boxes, gacha, gambling, sweepstakes on servers or in mods | Banned by the Creator Platform terms and Tebex rules | Store disabled, funds held | [16] |
| Third-party sponsorship or ads inside servers or mods | Banned by the Creator Platform terms and Mod Guidelines | Adverse action on the server | [10, 16] |
| Reselling other people's FiveM scripts or mods | Banned by the Creator Platform terms | Store closure | [16] |
| Running servers on alt:V, RAGE:MP or other unlicensed multiplayer frameworks | Both closed in 2026 after cease-and-desist letters; FiveM is the only licensed platform | Shutdown, legal letters | [20] |
| Porting GTA 6 map or story content into GTA V; story-expansion mods; ports to other platforms | Banned by the Mod Guidelines; a GTA 6-map-in-GTA-V project was struck | DMCA, legal action | [10, 25] |
| Paid mods sold outside Tebex / the Creator Platform | "Strictly non-commercial" outside approved storefronts | Takedown, legal letters | [10, 11] |
| Console jailbreak guides, hardware mods | Banned by the guidelines; anti-circumvention law | Legal exposure | [10] |
| Stat trackers that log into players' accounts, or scrape Rockstar services | No public API; credential sharing and automated access breach the Terms of Service | Account bans for users, legal letters | [26, 80] |

## Breaks platform, search or payment rules

| Idea | Why excluded | Main risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Networks of mass-produced, templated AI video channels (many channels, one template, one voice) | YouTube "inauthentic content" and "reused content" rules; cluster-level termination reportedly hit ~130,000 channels in six months | Termination of every linked channel; appeals reportedly rarely succeed (unverified) | [28, 32, 33] |
| Clip farms re-uploading streamers without substantive change, even with permission | Reused-content rule says permission does not exempt | Demonetisation, termination | [28] |
| Mass auto-generated, auto-translated or scraped guide sites | Google "scaled content abuse", including automated translation of scraped content | Deindexing | [37] |
| Buying views, followers, reviews, upvotes; account farms; agent-driven community spam | Fake-engagement and spam rules on every major platform | Bans, loss of the community you need | (platform rules; estimate) |
| Undisclosed sponsorships or affiliate links | Consumer-protection rules (for example US FTC endorsement guides, EU unfair-commercial-practices rules) and platform rules require clear disclosure of paid promotion | Fines, platform penalties, loss of audience trust | (estimate; check local rules) |
| Affiliate links to grey-market key resellers | Fraud and chargeback exposure; conflicts with affiliate-programme and payment terms | Account closure | (estimate) |
| Auto-checkout bots for consoles | Violate retailer terms and often payment terms | Order cancellations, account bans | (estimate) |
| "GTA 6 money-making" courses or "passive income" templates sold on hype | Not illegal as such, but the claims are what consumer-protection rules target, and the evidence in this guide doesn't support them | Refunds, platform removal, reputation | (estimate) |

## Fraud or impersonation (never)

| Idea | Why excluded | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fake giveaways and engagement bait ("free GTA 6 code", "PC beta key", "comment to win") used to farm follows, or giveaways you cannot fulfil | Deception; platform giveaway and spam rules; consumer law on prize promotions | (estimate) |
| Fake "GTA 6 download", "PC early access" or "playable build" pages and apps | Malware and fraud vectors; no such product exists | (reported) [22] |
| Impersonating Rockstar, Take-Two or official accounts; fake pre-order or "official mod marketplace" sites | Fraud and trademark law; payment-provider bans | (estimate) |
| Fake "GTA 6 RP server" pre-sales, whitelist fees or "founder packs" | There is no GTA 6 multiplayer or Cfx support to host one | [3, 17] |

## Not excluded, but not recommended

| Idea | Why | See |
|---|---|---|
| Patreon-funded single-player mods | Grey zone after the Sept 2026 guidelines; Patreon not named, "approved storefronts" is | `02` P5 |
| A single faceless AI-voiced channel with genuinely original, human-captured content | Legal if disclosed and original, but low probability and high classification risk | `02` V3 |
| Console resale | Legal, but thin, capital-hungry, taxable and time-boxed | `02` C2 |
| Selling ebooks with game screenshots | Commercial use of Rockstar material; text-only checklists are safer | `02` W6 |
| TTWO shares | Outside the scope of this guide; facts only, no advice | `01` section 4 |
