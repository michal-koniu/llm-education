# Sources

Compiled 30 October 2026. Numbers match the bracketed references in the guide.

How to read the quality notes:
- **primary**: the platform's, publisher's or company's own page or document.
- **press**: a news outlet reporting on a primary source.
- **analytics**: a modelled estimate from a traffic, streaming or channel-analytics vendor. Useful for magnitude, not exact values.
- **secondary**: a blog, a vendor with a commercial interest, or a search-result summary that could not be opened.

Rockstar's own support pages timed out while this guide was researched. Facts about Rockstar policy are relayed through press and should be checked on the live pages.
