### What that shape means for demand

A console-only, single-player launch creates demand for:

- story and mission walkthroughs, "missable" lists and ending guides;
- collectibles, maps, vehicle, weapon and property lists;
- "what the game doesn't tell you", settings, performance and 30 FPS explainers;
- news, reviews, reaction, lore and character analysis.

It does **not** create demand, in 2026, for:

- money-making guides for an online economy (none exists yet);
- roleplay (RP) servers (no GTA 6 multiplayer, no Cfx support announced);
- mods (no PC version).

The second wave (PC, an online mode, a creator platform) is a 2027 to 2028 event. GTA V's online mode arrived about two weeks after GTA V's launch, but the RP boom that drives GTA's current streaming economy only took off in 2019, six years later (reported) [41]. Plan for the second wave; don't build on it now.

### Lessons from earlier big launches

These are the closest data points for what launch traffic looks like. None is GTA. All are directional.

| Launch | What happened | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Elden Ring (25 Feb 2022) | Twitch launch day: 15.7M hours watched, 909,798 peak viewers [55]. First 3.5 days on Twitch: 51.8M hours, **183,670 channels streamed it** [54]. First five days, all platforms: 65.1M hours, peak just over 1M; the top 5 streamers had about 7.5M hours, one of them about 40% of that [56]. First 30 days: about 203M hours across Twitch, YouTube and Facebook [56]; "215M in just over a month" in another write-up of the same data [57] | (reported) |
| Elden Ring decay | Using the two Stream Hatchet figures, the first five days held about **32% of the first 30 days' hours watched** | (estimate from [56]) |
| Elden Ring guide sites | A large incumbent wiki reportedly had about 17M page views in the first month. A second source claims 180M, which looks like a unit error. The same wiki's whole domain shows 45M to 54M visits a month in late 2025 across all its games | (unverified [67]; estimate [66]) |
| Hogwarts Legacy (Feb 2023) | Single-player Twitch record: 1.28M peak concurrent viewers (Guinness, 9 Feb 2023) [59]. 13.7M hours on day one; 69.3M hours in the first week including three early-access days [60]. Twitch February total 71M hours from a different tracker, so the windows don't line up cleanly [61]. One creator claimed viewership fell from 1.2M to 16K within weeks [82]. 12M units and $850M in two weeks [65] | (reported; drop claim unverified) |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 (26 Oct 2018; story mode first, Red Dead Online beta from 27 Nov 2018) | Launch-week Twitch hours watched about **17M** (one Streamlabs post), half of the same month's big multiplayer shooter launch (34M), although both had similar numbers of streamers and hours streamed [62]. $725M of sales in three days [63] | (reported) |
| Baldur's Gate 3 (2023) | A community-run wiki shows about 11M to 12M visits a month in late 2025, two years after launch | (estimate, analytics tool) [66] |

Five lessons, all estimates drawn from the table:

1. **Supply spikes as fast as demand.** About 184,000 channels streamed Elden Ring in its first 3.5 days. GTA 6 will see more. Your launch-week content is one of hundreds of thousands of uploads.
2. **Attention concentrates.** In Elden Ring's first five days, one streamer had about 40% of the top five's hours. The launch spike mostly goes to people who already have an audience.
3. **Story-first launches decay fast and are watched less than multiplayer games.** RDR2, Rockstar's last big launch (story mode first; its online mode followed in beta about a month later), drew half the launch-week Twitch hours of a multiplayer shooter. People avoid spoilers and play the game themselves. Reference demand (maps, collectibles, "where is") lasts longer than watch demand.
4. **The tail is where a newcomer can win.** Elden Ring's month one was about three times its first five days. Reference sites are still busy years later. The money for a newcomer is in weeks 3 to 52, not week 1.
5. **A community wiki can beat incumbents** if it is clearly better and the community adopts it. That happened in one big 2023 launch. It takes community trust, not volume.

## 2. Rockstar and Take-Two rules

### 2.1 Video and streaming policy

- The long-standing policy ("Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material") was written for GTA V and carried forward. It was last updated 10 Jan 2025 (reported) [13]. It is a licence for **occasional, non-commercial** use by fans. "Non-commercial" means not making money through the footage (reported) [13].
- The 2013 text allows gameplay, full playthroughs with cutscenes and narrated Let's Plays. It bans pre-release or leaked footage (including unboxings), isolated cutscenes or endings, isolated in-game TV and comedy, **selling memberships that give access to the footage**, and **promoting your own merchandise** with the footage (reported) [14].
- In practice Take-Two has tolerated ordinary ad revenue on GTA V gameplay videos for 13 years. Guide channels have run since 2010 with billions of views (reported) [43]. No reported wave of claims against monetised gameplay was found. The honest position: **ad-monetised footage is tolerated in practice and unclear on paper**, and Take-Two can change that at any time.
- For the 27 Aug 2026 "Extended Look", Rockstar told creators they may react, clip, break down and stream watch parties with commentary, but "reposting the raw unedited video is not allowed" (reported) [15]. Expect the same line at launch.

### 2.2 Mod Guidelines (10 Sep 2026)

Five sections. Relayed by several outlets [10, 11, 12]:

- Single-player modding is tolerated. Multiplayer modding is limited to Rockstar's licensed Creator Platform (Cfx.re: FiveM and RedM).
- "You may only monetize your mods via digital storefront providers approved by Rockstar Games." Otherwise mods are "strictly non-commercial". Patreon is not named. That puts Patreon-funded mods in a grey zone, not a clear ban [79].
- Banned: real-money gambling, loot boxes, gacha, NFTs and crypto, selling virtual currency, monetising Rockstar's own assets; storyline expansions; mixing maps or characters across Rockstar titles; ports; cheat menus that touch official online services; third-party IP; Rockstar voice performances; real people's likeness without consent; using Rockstar marks to market mods or servers; third-party ads and sponsorships inside mods.
- The guidelines "are not a license". Rockstar can remove any mod "at any time and for any reason". GTA 6 is not named; outlets read them as covering future titles (inference by press).

| Case | What Take-Two did | Tag |

**Pattern:** permissive where Rockstar controls the money channel (Cfx, Tebex) and toward commentary and single-player mods. Aggressive on leaks, fakes passed off as real, ports, reverse engineering, trademark use and anyone who monetises Rockstar IP outside its channel.

### 2.5 Terms of Service essentials

Accounts cannot be sold, transferred or shared. Virtual items have no cash value and cannot be sold. Commercial markets for accounts or items are banned (reported) [26]. There is no public Rockstar API. Social Club was folded into the main Rockstar site in March 2026 (reported) [80]. Any tool that logs into a player's account or scrapes their data is unsafe.

## 3. Platform rules (2025 to 2026)

### 3.1 YouTube

| Rule | What it says | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Inauthentic content** (renamed 15 Jul 2025; the live page now heads this section "Generic or Repetitive Content") | Not monetisable: content that "looks like it's made with a template", "image slideshows ... or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative", and AI content "made with generic or unoriginal templates" that gives "the impression of mass production". Allowed: AI to edit scripts or make a unique visual, if the result shows "your creative vision" | (official) [28] |
| **Reused content** | Reaction and commentary are fine. Content copied "without any substantive modifications" is not, "even if you have permission from the original creator" | (official) [28] |
| Enforcement | 16 large AI-slop channels (35M subscribers, about $9.8M a year of estimated revenue) removed in Jan 2026. A Google paper describes cluster-level termination of about 130,000 channels in six months, judged on shared templates and infrastructure; the paper does not name YouTube and Google has not confirmed deployment. An "under 1% overturned" figure appears only in a newsletter summary | (reported; overturn rate unverified) [32, 33] |
| AI labels | YouTube began applying AI labels itself on 27 May 2026 | (reported) [31] |
| **Partner Program from 1 Feb 2027** | New applicants: 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days (today: 4,000 hours or 10M). Shorts pool payouts need 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days. Existing partners are grandfathered and must accept new terms by 31 Jan 2027 | (official for the deadline [29]; reported for the thresholds [30, 31]) |
| Gaming violence ad rules (Sep 2026) | Graphic game violence must not appear in the first seven seconds; some reports describe limited ads for violence in seconds 8 to ~15 and full eligibility after that. Shock montages, torture and sexual violence stay demonetised. Check YouTube's advertiser-friendly guidelines | (reported; sources conflict) [34] |
| Auto-dubbing | Free automatic dubbing into up to 20 languages, on by default for eligible channels, reviewable before publishing | (official) [35] |

Consequence: a new channel that wants the **old, lower bar must meet it and submit before 1 Feb 2027**, ten weeks after launch; how applications still pending on that date are treated is not documented. The violence rule is a tailwind for GTA footage if thumbnails and the first seven seconds are clean.

### 3.2 TikTok, Twitch, Kick

- TikTok Creator Rewards: 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, videos of 60 seconds or more (reported by blogs; TikTok's own page not read). Blogs conflict on fully AI-generated videos. Treat them as ineligible until you confirm otherwise (unverified).
- Revenue splits (gross): Twitch most partners 50/50; YouTube memberships 70/30 and long-form ads 55/45; Kick 95/5 on subscriptions; TikTok LIVE gifts about 50% nominal (reported) [36].

### 3.3 Google Search

- **Scaled content abuse:** making many pages mainly to manipulate rankings is spam "no matter how it's created". The policy names "automated transformations like synonymizing, translating" of scraped content (official) [37]. Translating **your own** original, checked guide is normal.
- **AI Overviews:** in a 2025 study of 68,879 searches, users clicked a result in 8% of visits with an AI summary and 15% without (reported) [38]. Another 2026 measurement found a 58% lower click-through rate for top-ranked pages on queries that showed an AI Overview (reported) [38]. Short factual game questions ("where is X") are among the most exposed. Interactive tools (a tracker with saved progress) are less exposed, because an AI summary cannot replace them.

### 3.4 Affiliate programmes

Amazon Associates pays 1% on physical games and consoles, 2% on digital games and 0% on gift cards (official) [39]. A $79.99 digital copy earns about $1.60. Game affiliate links are a rounding error.

Two launches are not a statistical sample. No verified closing price for the week of 30 Oct 2026 was found.
