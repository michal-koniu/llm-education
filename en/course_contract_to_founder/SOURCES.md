# Sources — From a B2B contract to a founder

All sources accessed **25 October 2026** unless stated otherwise. Labels:

- **[P]** primary: the law (Dziennik Ustaw / Sejm ELI API / ISAP), court judgments, official bodies (ZUS, GUS,
  KNF, PIP, gov.pl), the organisation's own page;
- **[S]** secondary: press, law-firm and adviser blogs, commercial templates;
- **[V]** self-reported by an investor, accelerator or company;
- **[A]** analysis made for this course from the primary texts — not a source, but a reading of one;
- **[R]** earlier files of this repo.

Statutes were read in their current official text through the Sejm ELI API. **DORA** was read from an unofficial
mirror because EUR-Lex refused automated access — check the wording on EUR-Lex (CELEX 32022R2554) before quoting.
Pages seen only as a search-engine excerpt are marked *(snippet)*.

## Weakest points — check these first

1. **DORA** was read from an unofficial mirror. Verify on EUR-Lex before quoting.
2. **Whether bank secrecy binds a lone IT contractor** who never sees client data — not settled.
3. **Whether DORA treats a sole trader as a provider or as personnel** — no official guidance; only commercial templates.
4. **The ryczałt rate for SaaS and for a configured box** — unresolved; ask your accountant.
5. **No statistics** yet on PIP reclassification decisions (only on interpretations).
6. **No Polish VC statement** on part-time founders, and **no Polish pre-seed valuation data** — field experiment 06
   fills this gap.
7. **"Data pewna" and timestamps** (part 03) and the **safety-buffer size** (part 04) are general knowledge / opinion.
8. **The net-income model** is simplified; your accountant's figures replace it.
